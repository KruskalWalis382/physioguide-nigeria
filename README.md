# PhysioGuide Nigeria

Build a complete, functional, mobile-first healthcare education website called PhysioGuide Nigeria. I am a beginner building from an Android phone and must finish within one day. Use a simple, maintainable HTML/CSS/JavaScript or equivalent lightweight structure. Do not add a backend, database, user accounts, paid integrations, external API keys or AI diagnosis.



PURPOSE: Help the Nigerian public understand common musculoskeletal pain, learn general safety information and know when to seek professional care. This is educational, not a diagnostic or treatment service.



DESIGN: Professional, calm, accessible healthcare design. Use navy blue, teal and white, readable typography, generous spacing, clear cards, accessible contrast and responsive mobile layouts. Include a simple logo using text, not a complex image. Optimise for Android phones and slower connections.



FEATURES:

1. A polished homepage with a clear headline, short introduction, navigation and clickable topic cards.

2. Three educational guides: low back pain, knee pain and neck pain. Explain common causes without claiming to diagnose, offer conservative general advice, and explain when professional assessment is appropriate. Do not make unsupported claims or promise cures.

3. An interactive educational safety checker with selectable answers about general symptoms and warning signs. It must never calculate a diagnosis, assign disease probabilities or recommend personalised treatment. For potential emergency warning signs, clearly advise urgent medical assessment. Include emergency warning information for new bladder or bowel dysfunction, numbness around the groin or saddle area, and new or progressive significant leg weakness in someone with back pain. Also flag major trauma, chest pain or breathing difficulty where relevant. Do not allow a reassuring result to override a warning sign.

4. A general movement information section with a few simple, low-risk examples and clear instructions to stop if symptoms worsen. Do not prescribe an exercise programme based on the checker.

5. A frequently asked questions section and a footer explaining the educational scope of the website.

6. A contact section with a WhatsApp link placeholder that I can edit later. Do not invent a telephone number. Do not include a fake submission form or claim messages have been sent.



FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS: Every navigation link, card, button and interactive control must work. The checker must display results based on the selected answers, prioritise warning signs and allow the user to restart. Include loading and error states only where actually needed. No fake accounts, fake testimonials, payment screens or nonfunctional controls.



SAFETY AND PRIVACY: Do not collect names, contact details, medical records or other identifiable health information. Do not send responses to a server. Clearly state that the website is not a substitute for professional care. Do not invent medical references or claim clinical validation.



DELIVERY: Create a finished working first version, not just a mockup. Make it publishable using the platform's free hosting option. Keep the implementation simple enough for a beginner to understand. After building, explain how to preview, test, publish and edit the website from a mobile browser. If any feature cannot be completed, tell me honestly rather than pretending it works.
